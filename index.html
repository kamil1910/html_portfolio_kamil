Our school

Where every learner is seen, heard, and valued.

We believe that deaf children deserve a rich learning environment that honors communication, identity, and achievement. Our educators combine high academic expectations with warm, individualized support so students can thrive socially, emotionally, and academically.

From early childhood education through middle years, we build foundations in communication, literacy, social-emotional learning, and confidence for a life of participation and success.
